Knightian uncertainty is a form of uncertainty that resists quantification; your unknown unknowns. 

Together, all your unknown unknowns form your **Knight Sky**. 

Despite its elusive nature, it's not an impenetrable space. A newborn's Knight Sky is larger than a grown adult's. Your exposure to Knight Sky weather is lower in isolation and familiarity than in complex, novel social encounters. 

When something utterly catastrophic rains down from the Knight Sky, entering our known knowns, potentially irreversibly obliterating us, we call that a **Knightmare**. 
When an entity flies out the Knight Sky, we call them a **Knight**. 
When we wish to bless the vast spectra of stars twinkling in the Knight Sky, we say to them, **"Knighty Knight."**

No soul - animate, inanimate, godly, mortal, or otherwise - is without a Knight Sky hanging over their heads. 

Your reality is always an open system.
